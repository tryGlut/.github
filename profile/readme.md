## 🔥 What is Glut?

**Glut** (German for *"ember"*) is a community-first, **non-dating** social app. No follower counts. No endless scroll. No swiping.

You join **small circles of up to 15 people** who share your interests, show up to real events together, and actually learn each other's names.

> *Big cities are full of people. They're not always full of your people.*

## ✨ What's inside

| | |
|---|---|
| 🫂 **Circles of 15** | Small enough that everyone matters. Big enough to feel like a crowd. |
| 🎟️ **Events & giveaways** | Real reasons to leave the house together. |
| 🏅 **Badges** | Recognition for showing up, not for posting. |
| ✅ **Verified members** | Protected verification badges, so you know who's real. |
| 🎨 **Profile cosmetics** | Frames, backgrounds, effects and accessories. *Visual only, no algorithmic matching bias.* |

## 🧭 Principles

```text
01  Circles, not audiences.        Max 15. Always.
02  Real life over screen time.    The app is the doorway, not the room.
03  Visual only.                   Cosmetics never change who you're matched with.
04  No dating.                     Friends, hobbies, weekends. That's it.
```
